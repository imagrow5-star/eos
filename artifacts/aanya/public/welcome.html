What Eos is

You don't need the right words, and you don't need to be in crisis to need somewhere to put it. Sometimes it just helps to say a thing out loud, to something that listens, remembers what matters to you, keeps it completely private, and never judges. Talk by voice or text, any hour. Eos remembers your goals and what you're going through, so over time it's less like starting over and more like talking to someone who actually knows you.

What Eos isn't

Eos is not a romantic partner, and it's not pretending to be a person. It's an AI, a friend to talk to, nothing more. If you're looking for romance or a relationship, this isn't the place, and that's okay. Eos is also not therapy or a substitute for professional help. It's simply a private space to be heard.

A friend in your corner

Not every conversation has to be heavy. Talk about your day, a small win, something on your mind, a goal you're chasing. Eos is here for the ordinary moments too. The kind of friend who remembers, who's glad when things go well for you, and who wants to see you grow.

Privacy

Your conversations belong to you. They're encrypted the moment they're saved, never sold, and never used to train AI. Private by design, because the whole point is a place you can be fully honest.
