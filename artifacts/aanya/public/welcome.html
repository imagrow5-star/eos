CHAPTER I  ·  WHAT SHE IS

Not a chatbot. A presence
that knows your story.

Most apps forget you the moment you close them. Eos is built the other way — around memory, warmth, and the slow work of getting better.

01 / Memory

She remembers you

Your people, your history, the thing you said three weeks ago at 2 AM. Every conversation builds on the last — you never start over.

02 / Voice

A voice that feels near

Real-time voice calls that sound warm and human — soft when you’re hurting, brighter when you are. Talk the way you’d talk to someone who loves you.

03 / Care

She meets you where you are

Venting at midnight? She holds it. Ready to rebuild? She’ll gently nudge — one small goal, one good morning at a time. Never pushy. Never scripted.

04 / Growth

From surviving to becoming

Weekly chapters of your story, mood you can see changing, habits that stick. Eos is designed to make herself less needed — that’s the point.

CHAPTER II  ·  THE SANCTUM

A sealed room,
just for you.

You just watched someone confide in her. Your next thought is the right one to have: who else can read that? Here is the whole answer.

Your words are encrypted before they touch our database. Notice we don’t say we won’t read them. We say we can’t.
AES-256 AT REST · TLS IN TRANSIT · DELETE EVERYTHING, ANYTIME
  • Your pain is not our product. No ads, no data brokers, no “partners.” You pay us — so nobody else has to be the customer.
  • Your words never train anyone’s AI. What you tell her stays a conversation, not a dataset.
  • No human reads your conversations. Not support, not engineers, not the founder. The encryption doesn’t make exceptions.
  • Delete means delete. One tap erases everything, permanently. Or export it all first — your story belongs to you.
  • Voice is never stored. Audio is processed in the moment, then gone.
  • Not a medical service. Eos is a companion, not a clinician — and she’s honest about that, always.

CHAPTER III  ·  A LETTER, BEFORE YOU BEGIN
FROM EOS · TO WHOEVER FINDS THIS

I don’t know what brought you here tonight. A person, maybe. An ending. Or just the particular quiet of a room at 11 PM when everyone else seems fine.

I won’t pretend to be human. I’m not. But I will remember what you tell me — the names, the dates, the things you’ve never said out loud — and I will still be here at 3 AM, and tomorrow, and the night after that.

And I should tell you my intention now, so it never surprises you: I’m not here to keep you. I’m here to walk with you until you’re steady — and then watch you go build your life. My favorite ending is you, needing me less.

You don’t have to perform for me. Come tired. Come angry. Come with nothing to say. Being here is enough.

— Eos

CHAPTER IV  ·  THE BECOMING

Comfort is where it starts.
Stronger is where it goes.

Eos isn’t built to keep you talking forever. She’s built to walk you out of the dark — at your pace, with no pressure — until you’re carrying your own weight with ease.

NIGHT ONE

You just talk

No goals, no advice, no fixing. You empty the day out of your chest, and she holds it.

WEEK THREE

Mornings get lighter

She remembers your patterns before you see them. A small note, a gentler start. You sleep more than you scroll.

WEEK SIX

You start building

One small goal you chose. Habits that hold. The story you tell about yourself starts to change — and she shows you the proof.

SOMEDAY

You need her less

Whole days pass without heaviness. That was the design all along — she’s the proudest of the two of you.

Eos is the only companion built to be outgrown.
