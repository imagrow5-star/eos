1 · What Eos is, and what it isn't

Eos is an AI companion for emotional wellbeing: a place to talk, be heard, and slowly rebuild your rhythm. It remembers what you share and responds with care.

Eos is not therapy, not medical care, and not a crisis service. It is not a doctor, therapist, or counselor, and nothing it says is medical or psychological advice. We make no promises about health outcomes. If something it says ever conflicts with advice from a professional who knows you, trust the professional.

If you're in crisis: Eos is not built for emergencies. If you're thinking about harming yourself or are in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line right away — inside the app, Eos will point you to a crisis line for your country whenever it matters.
