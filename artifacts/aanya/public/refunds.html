Refund Policy

LAST UPDATED · JULY 30, 2026

Fourteen days. Your money back. No questions, no forms, no convincing us.

The guarantee

If your first subscription payment doesn't feel right — for any reason, or no reason — tell us within 14 days of that first charge and we'll refund it in full. Email hello@eoscompanion.com, or use the link on the receipt Paddle emailed you. Either works.

Refunds are processed by Paddle, our payment partner and merchant of record, back to the payment method you used. Most refunds appear within a few business days, depending on your bank.

The trial

Every membership starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel any time before day 7 ends and you're never charged at all — nothing to refund, because nothing was taken.

Top-up packs

If we ever offer extra voice-minute packs, an unused pack is refundable within 14 days of purchase; a pack you've started using isn't. (These don't exist yet — this line is here so the policy won't change under you later.)

That's the whole policy

Eos exists for people going through hard seasons. Arguing with you over $19.99 would be a strange way to honor that. If something felt wrong, we'd genuinely like to hear about it — but you'll get your refund either way.
