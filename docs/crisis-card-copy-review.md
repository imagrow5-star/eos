Thank you for looking at this. These three sentences appear on a small card that
Eos, a private AI companion, shows when someone may be in a crisis. The card sits
directly under Eos's own reply and holds real helpline names and numbers, which are
never translated. Only these three lines are.

Line 1 is the card's first line when what the person said was clearly about wanting
to die or not wanting to be here.

Line 2 replaces line 1 when what they said could be read either way (for example a
philosophical question about the point of living). Just above the card, Eos has asked
them what they meant. So line 2 has to sit under an open question without answering
it: "whichever way you meant it, these are here if you ever want them."

Line 3 is the last line of the card, in Eos's own voice.

What matters is that each line reads as warm, plain and steady in your language, the
way a calm friend would say it. Not clinical, not alarming, not formal. Please change
anything that sounds translated, stiff or wrong in register, and keep the same meaning.
Informal "you" (tu / du / je / etc.) throughout. Gender-neutral wording where the
language allows it.

## English (the reference)

1. Someone who can be with you right now, if you want to reach:
2. Whichever it is — these are here if you ever want them:
3. I'm not going anywhere. Take your time.
