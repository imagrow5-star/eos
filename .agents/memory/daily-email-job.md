## What it is now

- **Package**: `@workspace/daily-email` at `artifacts/daily-email/` (name kept to avoid churn in the Render build filter)
- Deployed as a **Render Cron Job** `eos-hourly-sweeps`, defined in repo-root `render.yaml`, cron `0 * * * *`
- One job: POST three internal HMAC-protected endpoints on the api-server, in order — chapters, reflection, stories. The api-server owns every decision (windows, idempotency, writes).
- Needs only `APP_URL` + `SESSION_SECRET` (must equal the web service's). No DB, no encryption key, no model or email keys.
- Hooks: `SCHEDULER_DRY_RUN=1` (call nothing), `SCHEDULER_ONLY_USER=<id>` (scope to one user; a malformed id refuses to run rather than fanning out). Old `DAILY_EMAIL_*` names still honoured.

## What was retired, and why

Eos has no outbound channel — the landing page says no notifications, and an email in someone's inbox is the same push mechanic in a different channel. Removed:

- the morning email and the timed "you said 4 PM" commitment-nudge emails (the whole email pass of the old job, Resend + Claude usage in the job)
- web push entirely: `/api/push/*`, `/api/internal/push/morning-run`, `services/push.ts`, the `web-push` dependency, the client toggle in Chat settings, `public/sw.js`, and the "chapter ready" push from the chapter sweep
- the one-click unsubscribe route `GET /api/email/unsubscribe`

Kept, deliberately: transactional email in the api-server (verification, reset, account changes — account plumbing, not a push mechanic); the in-app morning note (generated by the chat route, never depended on the job); the columns `profile.daily_email_opt_out`, `profile.last_email_date`, `profile.push_opt_in` and the tables `push_subscriptions` / `push_events` (dropping needs a migration; deletion/export coverage stays).

The companion's capabilities block in `systemPrompt.ts` promises only the in-app morning check-in and states that nothing reaches the person outside the app — never re-add a channel there without the product decision changing.
